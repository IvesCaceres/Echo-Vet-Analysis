# Provenance and scope of the aggregate results

The seven hash-reviewed files in published/editorial/ were selected from previously verified aggregate artifacts produced by a read-only analysis of a private Gold snapshot. No clinical database was copied, and no new clinical query was required for their local incorporation. Their hashes are in published/editorial-manifest.json.

The bundle describes 3,364 Gold report records, 14,245 extraction-event rows, species/sex composition and syntactic presence of five fields. Records, animals, episodes and events are distinct units. Original reports and all individual-level derivatives remain private. Age/time summaries, diagnostic frequencies, clinical free text and individual records are not part of this editorial release.

The project owner confirmed that the responsible veterinarian permits publication of derived aggregate statistics, excluding original reports, clinical databases, individual records and identifiable information. This is an owner attestation, not independent verification of a source agreement or every possible additional right. The exact seven files were separately approved for the local candidate; remote publication is not authorized.

Original methods derive from the supplied local analysis project; tools/configuration, synthetic examples and publication controls were prepared separately. This is not an assertion of sole authorship of all prior methods or clinical interpretation. See LICENSING.md for the conditional MIT software proposal, which does not relicense clinical materials or automatically set editorial reuse terms.

Private input paths, clinical snapshot digests, reviewer identities, diagnosis records and private audit documents are deliberately not included here. Real figures were rendered from aggregate counts; no individual points, report text, case labels or private paths accompany them. Historical copies/linkage and selection/extraction limitations remain possible; see the exact methods in published/editorial/results.md.
